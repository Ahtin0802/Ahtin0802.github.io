  • 01 / History

Rich Historical Heritage

Explore History

As one of the oldest cities in central Taiwan, Changhua is home to profound historical sites. The city seamlessly blends traditional culture with modern living. From the majestic Baguashan Buddha overlooking the city to the beautifully preserved temples in the downtown area, every corner tells a story of the past. Walking through its vibrant streets offers a unique glimpse into the rich heritage that continues to shape the daily lives of its residents.

  • 02 / Food

Culinary Delights

Taste the Food

No visit to Changhua is complete without experiencing its incredible culinary scene. The city is famous nationwide for its traditional street food, most notably the Changhua Ba-wan (meatball) and slow-braised pork rice. These classic dishes, passed down through generations, attract food lovers from all over. Whether you are exploring local night markets or dining at a generations-old eatery, the local flavors are sure to leave a lasting impression.
